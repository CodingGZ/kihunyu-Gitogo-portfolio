About Kihunyu Gitogo

Early Life & Education

CPA John Kihunyu Gitogo is a seasoned finance expert, Certified Public Accountant (CPA-K), and governance specialist from Lobere, Githiga Ward in Laikipia County, Kenya. Raised in a humble rural background, he rose through local schools to earn both a Bachelor of Commerce (Finance) and a master's degree from the University of Nairobi, building a strong foundation in financial management and economic policy.

Professional Journey

Over the years, he has served in high-impact roles in leading organizations, including PricewaterhouseCoopers, KenolKobil, and the Coca-Cola systems in Kenya, Zambia, and Canada, gaining extensive experience in audit, corporate finance, and strategic planning. Today, he works as a finance consultant and independent governance professional, leveraging his expertise to design transparent, digitally driven public finance systems and to mentor the next generation of leaders.

Public Service Vision

As a Laikipia governor aspirant under the DCP Party, Gitogo channels his skills and experience into public service with a focus on digitalized own-source revenue systems, institutional accountability, and broad-based economic growth. He advocates for integrity and citizen-centered service delivery, ensuring that public resources translate into measurable improvements for households, businesses, and communities across all wards.

Our Mission

To protect life first through unbreakable security and accountable governance, then drive inclusive economic transformation that puts money directly into the pockets of Laikipia households.

We will achieve this by implementing the Laikipia Wealth Fund to give direct dividends to citizens, creating opportunities for youth and women, adding value to agriculture and livestock, building modern infrastructure, delivering quality healthcare and education, and ensuring transparent, disciplined and results-driven leadership that delivers tangible development felt at every household level.

Guided by the People's Manifesto, our leadership will be defined by unity, equity, discipline and execution—not slogans, but real action that restores dignity and builds lasting prosperity for all.

Our Vision

A secure, united and prosperous Laikipia County where every resident enjoys ownership of their future, lives in peace and safety, and directly shares in the wealth of the county.

We envision Laikipia as a model county in Kenya, a place where order has been turned into opportunity, public resources into direct citizen dividends, and our great potential into real shared prosperity for every family, ward and community by 2032.

Key Leadership Pillars

Shared Wealth

Ensure county revenue translates into equitable services and investments across all wards, with transparent mechanisms for planning and reporting.

Accountable Governance

Strengthen transparency in budgeting, procurement, and delivery of public projects to restore public trust and fight misuse of resources.

Economic Empowerment

Support youth, women, and SMEs with fair access to opportunities, markets, and county support programs that unlock local potential.

Social Services & Infrastructure

Prioritize health, education, water, and roads that directly improve daily lives and advance equitable development across Laikipia.

Community Engagements & Projects

Active involvement in community-driven initiatives and strategic projects that address the immediate needs of Laikipia residents while building sustainable development foundations.

Ward Development Forums

Conducting inclusive dialogues across all 15 wards to gather grassroots insights and co-design solutions:

  • Listen to ward-level challenges and citizen priorities
  • Map community-specific infrastructure and service needs
  • Build consensus on shared development opportunities

Youth Empowerment Initiative

Creating pathways for youth to build skills, access markets, and generate sustainable income:

  • Deploy vocational training and digital literacy programs
  • Link youth entrepreneurs to county procurement and market opportunities
  • Mentor emerging leaders and support startup ecosystems

Women & Cooperative Strengthening

Empowering women-led groups and savings societies to advance financial independence:

  • Strengthen financial management and governance in women's SACCOs
  • Create direct access channels to county procurement and investment opportunities
  • Support cooperative value-chain development and market linkages

Public Service Accountability

Building systems where county officials report on results and citizens shape decisions:

  • Establish real-time budget and project delivery dashboards
  • Create citizen feedback mechanisms for service quality monitoring
  • Conduct regular public performance reviews with ward representatives

Infrastructure Assessment

Sizing up Laikipia's critical gaps and prioritizing investments where need is greatest:

  • Conduct field visits to map road, water, healthcare, and education gaps
  • Validate community priorities with engineering and financial feasibility studies
  • Develop phased infrastructure roadmap aligned with county revenue capacity

Policy Advocacy & Reform

Developing research-backed policies that strengthen county governance and service delivery:

  • Collaborate with civil society and academia on evidence-based policy design
  • Reform budget processes and fiscal rules to improve resource efficiency
  • Advance digital systems and institutional accountability mechanisms across county operations
