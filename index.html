About Kihunyu Gitogo

Hon. John Kihunyu Gitogo, CPA-K, is a financial expert and governance consultant dedicated to transforming public finance into tangible development for the people of Laikipia County.

As a Laikipia governor aspirant under the DCP Party, he advocates for integrity, transparency, and citizen-centered service delivery, ensuring that public resources translate into real impact for households, businesses, and communities.

Focus Profile

  • Youth, Women & SME Empowerment
  • Inclusive Social Services & Infrastructure

Vision for Laikipia

Under Mheshimiwa Kihunyu Gitogo, Laikipia moves from a county where revenue is only collected to one where wealth is shared, opportunity is accessible, and every resident can thrive.

This vision puts people at the center of public finance, aligning county priorities with the day-to-day realities of farmers, traders, professionals, youth, and vulnerable groups across all wards.

Key Leadership Pillars

Shared Wealth

Ensure county revenue translates into equitable services and investments across all wards, with transparent mechanisms for planning and reporting.

Accountable Governance

Strengthen transparency in budgeting, procurement, and delivery of public projects to restore public trust and fight misuse of resources.

Economic Empowerment

Support youth, women, and SMEs with fair access to opportunities, markets, and county support programs that unlock local potential.

Social Services & Infrastructure

Prioritize health, education, water, and roads that directly improve daily lives and enable inclusive growth across Laikipia.
